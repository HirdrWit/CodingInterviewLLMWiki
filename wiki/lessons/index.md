Lessons teach the mental model and the pattern *triggers*. They never contain
solutions to problems in the set.

**Each lesson builds on the ones before it.** They are written to be read in
order — Sliding Window assumes Two Pointers, Graphs assumes Trees, Greedy is
framed against Dynamic Programming. Reading one out of order works, but you lose
the thread that makes the next one cheap.
