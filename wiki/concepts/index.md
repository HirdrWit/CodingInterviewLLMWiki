# Concepts

Atomic, reusable ideas. One idea per page — if it needs more than a screen, split it.

New pages here start from `templates/concept.md`.

Grouped by the [[../curriculum/index|curriculum]] topic that introduces them, but
the grouping is a filing convenience, not a fence — the whole point of a concept
page is that it gets reused somewhere else. `[[seen-set]]` is introduced in
Topic 1 and reappears in sliding window, graphs and backtracking.

**Status here is Rob's, not the page's.** A written page whose idea has never
been used under a timer is `unseen`. Promotion needs a linked solve — see the
ladder in [[../../CLAUDE|CLAUDE.md]].
