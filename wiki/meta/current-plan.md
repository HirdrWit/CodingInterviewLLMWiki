Everything else in the [[skill-tree|skill tree]] is parked. That's the right

## Week of 2026-09-10 — Topic 1, Arrays & Hashing

1. **Read [[../lessons/01-arrays-hashing|Lesson 1]]** end to end. 60–90 minutes.
   Don't skim section 3 — the mechanics are what make your complexity claims
   defensible under follow-up questions.
2. **Read the two GfG hashing articles** linked in section 9.
3. **Answer the section 10 self-checks aloud.** Aloud is not optional; it's the
   same muscle the interview uses.
4. **`timed contains-duplicate`** — the first 30-minute box.
5. **`debrief`** — show me the solution, finished or not.
6. Repeat 4–5 through the 8 problems in [[../curriculum/arrays-hashing|Topic 1]],
   in order. Roughly one Easy or one Medium per session.

**Target for the week:** problems 1–3 (the Easies) attempted and debriefed.
Not "solved" — attempted and understood. Speed comes from reps, not from rushing
the first one.

## Standing rules for this phase

- **One problem per box, one box per sitting.** Grinding six problems in a row
  teaches you far less than one problem plus a real debrief.
- **Never look at a solution before the box ends.** A problem you looked up is
  worth close to nothing as evidence, and it poisons the re-attempt.
- **Watch the NeetCode video only after the debrief.** It's a check on your
  reasoning, not a substitute for it.
- **Unfinished is fine.** It goes in the [[review-queue|review queue]] at +2 days.
  The plan is to repeat until cold-solvable, not to clear the list once.
